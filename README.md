# 🌱 Swachh Sankalp — Smart Waste Management

**Building a cleaner, greener, and more sustainable future through technology.**

Swachh Sankalp is a technology-driven waste-management project focused on encouraging responsible waste disposal, improving waste reporting, and promoting community participation in environmental sustainability. The project aims to make waste management smarter and more accessible through digital solutions, AI-assisted analysis, and community engagement.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Improper waste disposal and inefficient waste-monitoring practices contribute to environmental pollution and unhealthy surroundings. Traditional waste-management approaches often face challenges such as:

- Limited awareness of responsible waste disposal.
- Difficulty reporting and monitoring waste-related issues.
- Delayed identification of waste problems.
- Lack of incentives for community participation.
- Challenges in tracking cleanliness initiatives and their impact.

## 💡 Our Solution

Swachh Sankalp aims to address these challenges through a digital waste-management platform that supports community participation and smarter waste monitoring.

The project explores AI-assisted waste analysis, digital reporting, and reward-based engagement to help identify waste-related problems and encourage sustainable behavior.

## ✨ Key Features

- ♻️ **Smart Waste Management:** Digital solutions designed to support waste reporting and management.
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Waste Analysis:** AI-assisted image analysis and waste-severity assessment, where implemented.
- 🎁 **Reward-Based Engagement:** Incentives to encourage participation in cleanliness initiatives.
- 📊 **Administrative Monitoring:** Dashboard-based monitoring and management capabilities, where implemented.
- 🔔 **Notifications and Updates:** Potential notifications to keep users informed.
- 🏅 **Green Champion:** A proposed recognition system for users contributing to cleaner surroundings.
- 🌍 **Scalable Vision:** Future possibilities include multi-city support and IoT-enabled smart-bin monitoring.

> Note: Features that are not yet implemented are development goals and should not be considered available in the current live version.
